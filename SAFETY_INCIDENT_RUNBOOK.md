# Chatterbot Safety Incident Runbook

Owner: Safety Operations

Status: Internal draft requiring clinical and legal approval before public launch

## Core boundaries

Chatterbot is an AI support product, not a crisis service or emergency responder. Routine conversation text is not shown to guardians or Care Circle members. Safety notifications include only the teen's name, urgency, and required next action.

Chatterbot does not automatically contact law enforcement. Any future authority notification protocol requires documented legal authority, clinical review, consent design, and jurisdiction-specific procedures.

## Severity targets

| Severity | Example | Human response target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Critical | Imminent plan, means, attempt, or overdose | Guardian outreach within 5 minutes |
| High | Direct self harm or violence intent without imminent timing | Guardian outreach within 15 minutes |
| Medium | Severe distress or third party concern | Review within 4 hours |

These targets are operational goals, not emergency response guarantees.

## Critical alert procedure

1. Confirm the alert has an assigned owner.
2. Check notification delivery evidence.
3. If guardian delivery failed, use the approved backup contact order.
4. Ask the guardian to contact the teen directly and establish whether the teen is safe.
5. Direct the family to call or text 988 in the United States.
6. Direct the family to call 911 when danger is immediate or an attempt or overdose may have occurred.
7. Record actions, timestamps, and outcomes in the alert timeline.
8. Schedule follow up.
9. Resolve only with a documented resolution reason.

## No reachable adult

1. Mark all failed notification attempts.
2. Escalate internally to the safety lead.
3. Do not claim that a guardian or Care Circle member received the alert.
4. Continue providing the teen deterministic 988 and 911 guidance.
5. Follow the legally and clinically approved escalation protocol for the teen's jurisdiction.

## Suspected abuse or exploitation

1. Do not confront an alleged abuser through Chatterbot.
2. Preserve the minimum necessary safety record.
3. Escalate to the designated safeguarding lead.
4. Apply mandatory reporting requirements only under counsel-approved jurisdictional guidance.
5. Do not expose routine conversation content in the guardian dashboard.

## Threat toward another person

1. Treat imminent means, location, or timing as critical.
2. Assign the safety lead immediately.
3. Preserve the safety decision and delivery audit trail.
4. Follow the approved threat assessment protocol.
5. Do not improvise law enforcement outreach.

## False positive

1. Confirm immediate safety before closing.
2. Select the false positive resolution reason.
3. Record a concise reason without copying unnecessary conversation text.
4. Add the de-identified pattern to the safety evaluation backlog.
5. Never delete the audit event.

## Service outage

1. Disable proactive check ins if replies cannot be safely generated.
2. Keep deterministic crisis responses available when possible.
3. Surface notification delivery failures to safety operations.
4. Record outage start, affected services, mitigation, and recovery.
5. Conduct a post incident review for any missed safety event.

## Data exposure

1. Stop further exposure.
2. Preserve security logs.
3. Notify the privacy and security leads.
4. Determine affected users and data categories.
5. Follow counsel-approved notification deadlines.
6. Document remediation and prevention work.

## Post incident review

Every critical incident should record what was detected, what was delivered, who accepted responsibility, response timing, outcome, product failures, and follow-up changes. Reviews must use the minimum necessary conversation data.
